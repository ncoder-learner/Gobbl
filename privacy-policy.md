## 1. Introduction

FoodWrapped ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile app that lets you log meals, rate them, and share them with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your data.

By creating an account and using FoodWrapped, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

---

## 2. Who Can Use This App

FoodWrapped is intended for users **13 years of age and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has happened, please contact us at nealsinghal10@gmail.com.

## 3. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address** — used to sign you in and communicate with you
- **Display name** — the name shown on your profile
- **Username** — your unique handle in the app
- **City** — optional, user-entered; displayed on your profile if you choose to provide it

We do **not** collect your precise GPS or device location.

### User Content

When you log meals, we collect:

- Food photos you take or upload
- Meal names
- Ratings and scores you assign to meals
- Notes and captions you write

### Social Data

When you use the social features, we collect:

- Friend connections and friend requests you send or receive
- Likes and comments you give or receive on posts

### Device Information

- **Push notification token** — used solely to deliver push notifications to your device (for example, when a friend likes your post or accepts a friend request). You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or within the app.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account
- Display your profile and meal log to you and, where you choose, to your friends
- Enable social features — friends, posts, likes, and comments
- **Automatically identify food in your photos using AI** — when you upload or take a food photo, it is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to identify the food in the image. This result is used to populate your meal log. See Section 5 for details.
- Send push notifications about social activity (likes, comments, friend requests) if you have enabled them
- Respond to your support and privacy requests

We do **not** use your data for advertising purposes. We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 5. Who We Share Your Information With

We share data only with the service providers listed below, solely to operate the app. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

### Supabase

We use **Supabase** for database storage, user authentication, and photo storage. Your account information, meal data, social data, and uploaded photos are stored on Supabase's infrastructure.  
Privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

### Anthropic (Claude AI)

When you log a meal, your **food photo is transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API**, which identifies the food in the image. The result is returned to the app and used to populate your meal log entry. We encourage you to review Anthropic's privacy policy for details on how they handle API inputs.  
Privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

### Expo

We use **Expo** for push notification delivery. Your device's push notification token may be shared with Expo's notification infrastructure for this purpose.  
Privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

### Google and Apple

If you sign in using **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple**, your sign-in is handled directly by those services. We receive only the minimum information needed to create your account (typically your email address). Please review their respective privacy policies for details on how they process your sign-in data.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

### Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the app: go to **Profile → Account → Delete Account**. Deletion removes your profile, meal history, posts, comments, likes, and friend connections.

### Updating Your Information

You can update your display name, username, city, and other profile details from within the app at any time.

### Data Access or Deletion Requests

To request a copy of your personal data, or to request deletion if you are unable to access the app, email us at **nealsinghal10@gmail.com**. We will respond within a reasonable time.

### Push Notifications

You can enable or disable push notifications at any time in your device settings or in the app under **Account → Notifications**.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. User data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure, which uses row-level access controls so that users can only access their own data and the data of their accepted friends. Photos are stored in access-controlled cloud storage.

No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for any account you use to sign in.
