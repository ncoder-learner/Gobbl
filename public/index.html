The loop

Three taps a meal.

No calorie diary, no forms. Just log what you ate and let Gobbl do the sorting.

01

Snap it, name it, rate it

Photograph the meal and Gobbl fills in the name and cuisine for you. Drag the slider to score it, mark it home or out, and you're done.

02

Watch your rankings settle

Every meal lands in your personal tier list for the month, ranked against everything else you've eaten, not a stranger's average.

03

Let a friend settle it

When two of you post the same meal, Gobbl turns it into a duel. Votes come in live until it closes, whoever's plate wins, wins.
